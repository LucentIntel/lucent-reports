03

Why that gap matters more heading into 2027

Google Search still works well for patients who already know what they want. But it works like an auction. Every click on search terms like “weight loss clinic near me” and “hormone therapy Chicago” already costs $15 to $35, before anyone even becomes a patient. That price goes up as more clinics compete for the same words. And more clinics keep showing up.

Facebook and Instagram work differently. They reach people before they even know they have a problem. Being seen there builds trust for free. By the time someone does search on Google, they are not comparing five strangers by price. They already know your name. That kind of trust cannot be bought with Google ads alone.

There is one more twist. Google treats weight loss and hormone topics as sensitive. Because of this, Google will not let you show reminder ads to people who already visited your site. Most businesses do this all the time. A furniture store can show you the same couch ad for weeks after you look at it. Your clinic legally cannot do that. So Google cannot help keep your name in front of people after they visit. Something else has to do that job instead.

04

The other half: what happens after someone reaches out

You already built something most clinics never do. Before anyone pays $3,900 or more for a full program, they take a smaller first step: a $197 Clarity Call or a $997 Metabolic Snapshot™. That's smart, since one patient here can be worth $3,000 to $15,000 or more over time. But what happens to people who do not book right away? Most people need about six contacts before they decide to buy something, not just one. For clinics like yours, about 40 out of 100 calls come in after 5 PM, when no one is there to answer. If a caller gets voicemail instead of a real person, most do not wait around. About 85 out of 100 will just call the next clinic on their list instead. None of this has to mean a lost patient. Clinics with automatic follow-up win back 15 to 20 more patients out of every 100 who did not book right away. But here is the catch: a DM, a missed call, an email, and a website form all land in different places. No one is watching all of them at once to see who is ready now and who just needs a nudge later. And since Google will not remind these people for you, someone or something else has to.

05

The market isn't waiting

We checked Facebook's public ad records ourselves. Options Medical Weight Loss, a clinic close to Lincoln Park with similar services, is running about 150 ads right now. Better Med Spa & Wellness, also in Lincoln Park, is running 3 ads, and one of them started less than a month ago. Two other clinics like yours, Peacock Health Collective and Innovative Vitality, are running zero ads. This shows something important: the clinics that show up get noticed. The ones that don't, don't.

As more clinics start running ads, having the best reviews will not be enough. Everyone will have good reviews. What matters most is timing: who is already in a patient's mind before they even start typing, and who reaches out the moment that patient is ready.

If someone doesn't hear back fast enough, they don't just disappear. They search again, and the next clinic down the road answers instead.
