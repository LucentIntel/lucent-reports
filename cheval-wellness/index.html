01

What's already working in your favor

4.9 stars across 157 Google reviews is a genuinely strong number, stronger than most practices in your market this week, including the ones spending real money on paid marketing right now. That reputation earns you the tie-breaker with anyone who's already comparing options. The sharper question is how many people never get far enough to read it.

02

We pulled up your public presence the way a prospective patient would: Google, then Facebook, then Instagram. Google looks intentional, since you're showing in a sponsored position for local search. Facebook and Instagram tell a quieter story.

71% of patients say a provider's social presence is a real factor in who they choose, and roughly 3 in 10 local searches involve social media somewhere in the journey. Not as the starting point, but as where people size someone up before they commit.

Right now Cheval is paying to win the moment someone finally searches, and showing almost nothing to the much larger number of people who haven't started searching yet.

03

Why that gap matters more heading into 2026

Google Search still wins the patient who already knows what they want, but it's an auction. Cost-per-lead on terms like "weight loss" or "semaglutide near me" already runs $15 to $35 a click before anything converts, and that number climbs as your local market gets more crowded. It is.

Meta works on a different mechanic: it reaches people before they've decided they have a problem worth solving. A real presence there builds familiarity for free, so by the time someone does search, they're not comparing five strangers on price. They already recognize your name. That's being known before you're needed, and no amount of Google spend buys it.

04

The other half: what happens after someone reaches out

You've already built real discipline with the Clarity Assessment™. The open question is whether every door reaches it the same way: a Sunday-night DM, a call missed at 6:45pm, a call that lands mid-consult and competes with the patient in the room. With a patient worth $3,000 to $15,000+ over the relationship, one of those slipping through isn't a small detail. It's revenue, and a front-end system's job is simply to make sure all three doors feed the Clarity Assessment™ the same way, with warm leads surfaced first.

05

The market isn't waiting

Titan Medical Center, Peaks of Health Metabolic Medical Center, ExcelLife Integrative Care, and Invigorate Wellness Medical are all actively running paid marketing in your footprint right now, four of a growing list of wellness and hormone-focused practices within a few miles of you.

As more of them turn on paid marketing, winning stops being about who has the best reviews. Everyone will. It comes down to timing: who's already top of mind before the prospect starts typing, and who catches them the moment they raise their hand.

Because if they don't hear back fast enough, they're not gone. They're one search away from the clinic down the road.
