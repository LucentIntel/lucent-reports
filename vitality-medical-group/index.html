04

Why that gap matters more heading into 2027

Google Search still wins the patient who already knows what they want, but it's an auction. Cost-per-lead on terms like “medical weight loss near me” and “semaglutide Lake of the Ozarks” already runs $15–$35 before anything converts, and that number climbs as your local market gets more crowded. It is.

Meta works on a different mechanic: it reaches people before they've decided they have a problem worth solving. A real presence there builds familiarity for free, so by the time someone does search, they're not comparing five strangers on price. They already recognize your name. That's being known before you're needed, and no amount of Google spend buys it.

When we talked, you mentioned that patients can reach Vitality Medical Group either by calling in or by filling out the online form — and that most of them call. That lines up with Gap 1 above: your busiest, highest-converting channel is also the one with the least visible backup.

None of this means the phone is the wrong channel — it’s clearly the one patients trust. It means the channel doing the most work right now is also the one most exposed if a call gets missed, and that exposure isn’t a small edge case: on the numbers above, a real chunk of the people trying to reach Vitality Medical Group are calling at a time nobody’s there to answer. That's not a speed problem you can fix by having someone answer faster during the day — it's a coverage gap, and it's exactly what a nurture system built to catch and follow up on after-hours contact is for. For a patient worth $3,000–$15,000+ over the course of treatment, that single point of failure is the whole ballgame.
